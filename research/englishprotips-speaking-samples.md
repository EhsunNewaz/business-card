## 2) Part 1 — Tell me about the house… (Lesson 2, full)

- Video alt: …/3bfbabd7-cc24-4d29-a6be-a2f7b75110b2 · PDF login-walled.
- **Transcript:** "Well, I live in a beautiful old apartment on the outskirts of
  Edinburgh. It's a relatively large apartment, which I share with my partner. There
  are two bedrooms, a living room and an open-plan kitchen — which is where I spend
  most of my time." (Fill-in targets: outskirts, relatively, share, plan, spend.)
- **Vocab banks:** home (spacious house, cosy flat, bright/cheerful…) · location
  (outskirts, centre, residential neighbourhood, east side) · who-with (share with
  partner/flatmates, live alone, parents, dormitory, halls) · layout (open-plan
  kitchen, dining room, balcony overlooking…).

## 3) Part 1 — Have you lived here long? (Lesson 3, full)

- Video alt: …/669b2b9a-0b3a-4c99-b006-68c895d6c65f · PDF login-walled.
- **Transcript:** "I've been living in my apartment for the better part of two years.
  I used to live in the city centre, but to be honest, I found it a bit loud and
  expensive. I'm much happier living on the outskirts of the city where it's quieter."
  (Fill-in: been, better, used, in, found, much, where.)
- **Grammar points:** present perfect continuous · used to + infinitive · find sth +
  adjective · much happier + verb-ing.

## 6) Part 3 — City or countryside for children? (Lesson 49, full)

- No video embed on page · PDF login-walled.
- **Transcript:** "Personally, I think both have advantages and disadvantages. City:
  exciting, exposes children to people/experiences, but more dangerous (traffic,
  strange people). Countryside: safer but too sheltered, fewer life experiences. I
  guess it depends a lot on which city…" (Fill-in: Personally, expose, dangerous,
  strange, contrast, sheltered, depends.)
- **Vocab (6 + examples):** cost of living · sense of community · slower pace of life ·
  better educational opportunities · meet people from all walks of life · greater
  access to healthcare and education.
