P2: Japanese novel "Mamiya Store" (~400–500pp, translated): 3 thieves hide in empty house, letter from the PAST in milk box… (forgot title/author — handled honestly; stopped by time; recommends: mystery, hidden clues, life lesson).
P3: fiction = escape/fantasy/relaxation / TV-vs-books "lazy" criticism: agree+disagree (sister = book person; both teach; TV adds listening) / books grow imagination (no visuals → own images) / e-books ± (eye strain, less read; cheap + saves paper) / banned books (propaganda/truth-hiding; no right to ban BUT may prevent violence from strong-headed people).
Feedback: scored 8/8/8/9 ≈ Band 8, near 9. Errors: "cityside"→city; "one jewellery"→one piece of; "box of milk"→carton/container (contextually OK); "a television"→the television; "vocabularies"→vocabulary (uncountable). Praised: live-concert paraphrase, "top of the singing industry" (for charts), phrasal verbs (running out of), informal idiom (book person, in the spotlight), REAL unmemorised P2 + storytelling (tenses+vocab+engagement), both-sides critical thinking. Note: average native speaker = 7.5!
Background: international school since Y1 (all-English, secret Thai!), prefers English for academic topics; Japanese (cram school → 8-mo intensive → entered uni a year EARLY); Korean for K-dramas (grammar ≈ Japanese); learning via Netflix + English subs (whisk, saucepan, gastronomy, fondant); no vocab book — repetition ("see a word 7× in context"); thinks IN the language, never translates (too slow for conversation); theme-sticking (baking shows). Tip: exposure + daily use + environment; get the LEVEL first, scores follow.
