## 3) Form completion (30 min, full)

- Video alt: https://iframe.mediadelivery.net/play/220384/ba15d505-3900-4a98-95ad-4d1bf81b12cc
- Lesson PDF (publit, untested): IELTS-Listening-Form-Completion-Packham-s-Shipping
- **Material:** Part 1 is usually form completion (2 speakers, M+W). **Strategy:**
  1. read word-limit instruction (1 word / 2 words / number / combos) · 2. understand
  + underline form keywords pre-recording · 3. predict answers (surname, college,
  postcode, measurements, box items, money) · 4. answers in order — keep reading ahead
  so one miss doesn't cascade · 5. expect answer bursts (2 answers in 10 seconds —
  scribble fast, transfer later).
- **Pro tips:** numbers ('oh' for 0; double/triple; 'oh' = letter O in alphanumeric
  codes) · letters (surname/place spelling) · address format (number/street/town/
  postcode e.g. SW9 9SZ).
- **Practice:** Packham's shipping agency · Ordering stationery
  (englishprotips.com/listening_questions/…).
