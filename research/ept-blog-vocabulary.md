## 1. My 10 Favourite IELTS Expressions (2026-07)
URL: https://ielts.englishprotips.com/my-10-favourite-ielts-expressions/
📄 https://ielts.englishprotips.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/My-10-Favourite-IELTS-Expressions.pdf
1. **To some extent** — partly, not completely. *"I do, but only to some extent. Space exploration can lead to valuable advances, but should not take priority over healthcare and education."*
2. **It comes down to** — the deciding factor is. *"I think it ultimately comes down to personality."*
3. **For instance** — for example. *"There are lots of reasons. For instance, some need English for careers..."*
4. **Having said that** — introduces the other side. *"Working from home offers flexibility. Having said that, office makes collaboration easier."*
5. **On the one hand / On the other hand** — two sides. *"On the one hand homework helps practise; on the other hand too much leaves less family time."*
6. **If** — condition. *"If you use your phone for work, hours are understandable; if you check social media for no reason, it's a bad habit."*
7. **The thing is** — clarifies real meaning. *"The thing is, it's difficult to give a simple yes or no."*
8. **It depends** — answer varies by factor. *"It depends on what you are looking for."*
9. **Tend to** — often but not always. *"People tend to shop online because it's convenient."*
10. **Strike a balance** — compromise. *"I think it is important to strike a balance."*
Quiz (10Q) answers: extent / comes down to / For instance / Having said that / On the one hand+other hand (drag) / If / The thing is / It depends on / tend to / strike a balance.

## 2. 20 Simple Vocabulary Swaps To Improve Your IELTS Writing Score (2026-05)
URL: https://ielts.englishprotips.com/20-simple-vocabulary-swaps-to-improve-your-ielts-writing-score/
📄 https://ielts.englishprotips.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/20-Vocabulary-Upgrades-for-IELTS-Essays.pdf
Intro: Lexical Resource = 1 of 4 criteria; swaps make writing academic/precise.
1. agree/disagree → **completely/partially agree** (nuance)
2. for example → alternate **for instance**
3. problem → **issue** (esp. problem-solution essays)
4. good → **beneficial**
5. bad → **harmful to** (not "for")
6. think → **believe**
7. people who agree → **advocates**
8. people who disagree → **critics**
9. a lot of → **a large proportion of**
10. cause → **result in**
11. advantages → **benefits**
12. disadvantages → **drawbacks**
13. In my opinion → **I would argue** (max 1–2×/essay)
14. stop → **prevent**
15. help → **contribute to**
16. because → **due to** (+ noun phrase, NOT clause)
17. big/large → **significant** (trends/effects, not size)
18. small → **marginal** (slight changes, not size)
19. money → **funds** (collocation: allocate funds)
20. skills → **practical/soft/hard skills** (practical=everyday, soft=social, hard=technical)
Quiz (20Q): issue / believe / beneficial / harmful / for instance / critics / advocates / a large proportion of / result in / benefit / drawback / I would argue that / prevent / contribute to / due to / significant / marginal / funds / practical skills / partially agree.

## 3. 15 Words for IELTS Essays (2026-04)
URL: https://ielts.englishprotips.com/15-words-for-ielts-essays/
📄 https://ielts.englishprotips.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/15-vocabulary-words-phrases-ielts-task2-essays-band7.pdf
1. **I would argue that** — formal opinion (*"...the benefits of cheaper public transport outweigh the downsides."*)
2. **Many people believe that** — others' opinion
3. **Tend to** — generally true (*"Young people tend to spend more time online."*)
4. **For instance** — variety with "for example"
5. **Due to** — formal reason + noun phrase (*"Due to high housing costs..."*)
6. **By contrast** — stronger than "however" (*"English students pay £9,000. By contrast, Scottish students are subsidised."*)
7. **As a result of** — cause/effect, flexible position
8. **Ensure** — formal "make sure"
9. **Enable** — formal "allow" (positive outcomes; *"Scholarships enable poorer students to study abroad."*)
10. **Individuals** — formal "people", alternate for range
11. **Lack** — concise "not having enough" (*"Many developing countries lack resources..."*)
12. **Invaluable** — extremely valuable
13. **Paramount** — top priority (*"Education is paramount for a successful career."*)
14. **Significant** — upgrade of big/large
15. **If** — conditionals show grammar range (*"If the government provides scholarships, more will attend."*)
Quiz (15Q): I would argue that / Many people believe that / tend to / For instance / Due to / By contrast / As a result of / ensure / enable / individuals / lack / invaluable / paramount / significant / If.

## 6. 5 Essential Phrases for IELTS (2026-03)
URL: https://ielts.englishprotips.com/5-essential-phrases-for-ielts/
📄 https://ielts.englishprotips.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/5-essential-phrases-ielts-speaking-score.pdf
1. **Tend to** (+ base verb; neg: don't tend to). Adv tip: + contrast (*"Young people tend to be liberal, while older people are conservative"* — young/old, rich/poor, children/parents, men/women). Practice Qs: commute / after work / newspapers.
2. **Depends on** (never "of"; strengthen: really/largely). + contrast to extend. Practice Qs: eat out vs cook / children early vs late / big cities.
3. **If** — all 4 conditionals: zero (*"If people work from home, they don't commute"*), 1st (*"If companies use AI, workers will lose jobs"*), 2nd (*"If I could live anywhere, I'd live in Canada"*), 3rd (*"If they hadn't helped, I don't know what I would have done"*). Practice Qs: WFH preference / AI future / where to live.
4. **For example / For instance / Such as** (such as = mid-sentence + gerund). Practice Qs: environment / parents & reading.
5. **To some extent** — partial agreement (*"I think that is true to some extent..."* + both sides). Openers: I agree / This is true / That's correct + to some extent. Practice Qs: advertising / technology & sociability.

## 18. Advanced IELTS Vocabulary for Academic Task 1 — 5 Key Phrases (2021-02)
URL: https://ielts.englishprotips.com/advanced-ielts-vocabulary-for-academic-task-1/
🎬 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o1Nbc-RY4PI (5 Key Phrases for Academic Task 1)
1. **Overall,** — flags the overview (comma required). *"Overall, from 2008 to 2018, the percentage of hybrid and fully electric vehicles... increased significantly..."*
2. **meaning that / This means that** — depth of analysis (*"...increase to 32%, meaning that it will be the most common vehicle type in 2028"*).
3. **respectively** — data from 2+ categories (*"...increase to 32% and 30%, respectively"*).
4. **albeit** — contrast ≈ although (*"...expected to increase, albeit not as much as..."*).
5. **Compared to** (+ sentence-initial = automatic complex sentence; Grammatical Range).
