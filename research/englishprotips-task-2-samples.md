## 3) Types of essay question (20 min, full)

- Video alt: https://iframe.mediadelivery.net/play/220384/8458641f-3a24-4a02-ad12-3db8a2ff470f
- PDF (login): task-2-practice-essays-questions.pdf
- **5 types + rules:** Agree/disagree (opinion in intro+conclusion; one-sided OK —
  e.g. regular school tests) · Advantage/disadvantage (which outweighs? —
  e.g. physical shops → online) · Discussion (both sides + your view —
  e.g. social networking) · Double question (1 paragraph per Q — problem+solution /
  cause+solution / reasons+advantages, e.g. rural→urban migration, endangered
  animals, family living far apart) · Direct (rare — e.g. who should fund medicine
  research).
- **Quiz (10 Q, identify the type):** children later→adv/dis · overscheduled kids
  (good? consequences?)→double · environment = govts not individuals→agree ·
  kids' phone games→adv/dis · study abroad pos/neg→adv/dis · foreign language at
  school→discussion · city pollution fee→discussion · which natural areas + outweigh?
  →double · parental pressure→discussion · food waste reasons+solutions→double.
