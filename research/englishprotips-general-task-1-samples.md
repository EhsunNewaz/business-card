## 1) Lesson 1 — Aiming for 7+ (30 min, quiz full)

- Video alt: https://iframe.mediadelivery.net/play/220384/5fa15386-4ffc-44b8-9df0-f7fe122f491b
- PDF (login): introduction-to-general-task-1-faqs.pdf
- **Quiz (15 Q):** writing test time? → 1 hour · T1 time? → 20 min · 3 letter styles →
  formal/informal/semi-formal · words? → more than 150 · semi-formal to? → known but
  not close · style if: friend→informal, hotel manager→formal, friend re job→formal,
  professor→formal · letter order → Greeting, Opener(purpose), Bullet 1–3, Closer,
  Sign-off · name at bottom? → yes · formal greeting → "Dear Sir or Madam," ·
  sign-off for Sir/Madam → Yours faithfully · semi-formal greeting → "Dear Mr Lewis,"
  · sign-off for Mr Lewis → Yours sincerely. (Includes joke distractors:
  "Dear squires and mad men", "Keep it real".)

## 2) Lesson 2 — Formal vs. Informal Letters (30 min, full)

- Video alt: https://iframe.mediadelivery.net/play/220384/b23262aa-2423-4c10-8f5c-c6ad0d29a2b1
- PDF (login): the-8-differences-between-formal-and-informal-letters.pdf
- **Material (short — lesson is video-led):** informal letters open with chit-chat
  before the purpose. Expressions: How are you? / How have you been? / I hope you're
  well. / What's been happening with you? / How are the kids? / How's the family? /
  How's life? Have you been up to much recently? / I'm sorry it's been so long since
  I last wrote. / Thought I'd just send a short letter… / I've been meaning to write
  for ages.
