- Video alt: …/0595cfb9-bf8d-4f10-9b71-627be6b65751
- Qs: handshake? (no, unless examiner initiates) · duration (11–14 min) · parts (3) ·
  part 1/2/3 formats · common P1 topics (home/hometown/neighbourhood/studies/job) ·
  don't-know phrases (multi: "I don't know if I'm the right person…", "tricky
  question…", "never really considered…") · REE = Respond, Elaborate, Example ·
  clarification ("Sorry, could you repeat…?"; "what does ___ mean?") · 6× do/don't
  (eye contact ✓, elaborate ✓, watch real tests ✓, long pauses ✗, memorise ✗, fear
  interruption ✗) · P2: tell a story; if stuck → talk future; talk till stopped;
  practise by phone-recording, same topic repeatedly + new vocab · P3 = opinions ·
  end with "Thank you very much", no feedback ask.

## 11) Agree-Disagree Essay (25 min) — 2 structures + 2 models (full)

- Video alt: …/bc0fa232… · Lesson PDF via publit/ReadingCourse (untested).
- **Basic (fully agree/disagree):** shorter, T2-light. Model: teens should have
  regular exams (practice for HE assessment + work skills: time management, resiliency,
  delivering under pressure, meeting deadlines). Fill-in: be given · they · Having ·
  to continue · Hence · prepares · under · meet · In · because.
- **70/30 (mainly one side):** 3 arguments (2+1), longer, shows critical thinking +
  range. Same topic + counter (exam-focus → 'teaching the test', weak critical
  thinking) → conclusion template: "While I acknowledge that…, I would argue that…
  because…". 323 words. Fill-in: is · whether they · ensures · further · be tested ·
  range · taking · Those · argument · rather · As a result · lack · while ·
  teenagers · range.
