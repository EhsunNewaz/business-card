## 1) Lesson 1 — Introduction to Task 1 (15 min, full)

- Video alt: https://iframe.mediadelivery.net/play/220384/37ac6870-a0cd-459c-bcc3-6e2c9bc235c6
- PDF (login): introduction-to-academic-task-1-faqs.pdf · Example graphic (source: ielts.org, image)
- **Quiz (6 Q):** write about? → report based on a graphic · time? → 20–25 min ·
  weight? → 1/3 (T1) + 2/3 (T2) · words? → 150 minimum · what stops band 6+? →
  wrong/no overview · definitely NOT do? → explain the reason for the trend.
- **FAQ (12):** 2 parts (report/letter + essay) · General = migration/letter, Academic =
  university/report · paper vs computer (same test; typing faster) · bring only
  pens/pencils/eraser + ID, no phones/watches · L/R/W same day, Speaking ±7 days ·
  plan on question paper (recommended) · read T2 first but START T1 (brain incubates
  ideas; 20–25 min on T1 since students score lower there) · no upper word limit ·
  no conclusion needed, overview required · graphic types: table, line, bar, stacked
  bar, pie, process, map (+ combos) · T2 worth double, but focus T1 for gains.

## 3) Lesson 3 — How to write a Task 1 introduction (15 min, full)

- Video alt: https://iframe.mediadelivery.net/play/220384/a0e3e4e7-a8d3-451f-a4f8-f891bbdf10a7
- PDF (login): how-to-write-the-perfect-introduction-cheat-sheet.pdf
- **Material:** intro = re-worded rubric (1–2 sentences, max 3 min). 3 moves:
  1. synonyms (shows→presents), 2. different grammar (who went abroad→studying abroad),
  3. rephrase time (between 2005–2015 → from 2005 to 2015 / over the decade / over a
  10-year period). **Don'ts:** unsure synonym → copy rubric instead; never paraphrase
  'below'; no axis measurements.
- **FAQ (3):** don't paraphrase 'below' · no need to paraphrase ALL words — speed first ·
  max 3 minutes on intro.
- **Quiz (4 Q):** paraphrase 'from 2010 to 2018' (multi: between 2010 and 2018; over an
  8-year period) · paraphrase 'until 2050' (up to 2050 / till 2050) · 2× drag-drop intro
  builders (butter/milk table; pie charts of languages 2000 vs 2010).
