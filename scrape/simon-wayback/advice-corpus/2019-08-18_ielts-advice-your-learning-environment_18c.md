# IELTS Advice: your learning environment
- **Date:** 2019-08-18 | **আর্কাইভ লিংক:** [2019/08/ielts-advice-your-learning-environment.html](https://web.archive.org/web/2024/https://www.ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2019/08/ielts-advice-your-learning-environment.html) | **Comments:** 18 (টপ ১০ #৪)

## Content
Yesterday I gave some advice about putting yourself in an English language environment. But how do you do that? How can you improve your environment so that it accelerates your learning?

Several people asked these questions in the comments below yesterday's lesson, so let's have a discussion: what do you think?

## Comments (18)
1. **lnr** (2019-08-18): I used to take a speaking course with a native speaker, but he never let students speak more than two or three sentences and was rather lenient. Soon I felt like I was in a lecture hall. After several disappointments I gave up looking for a person interested in my improvement and started preparing on my own. Anything is better than wasting money and time on teachers that don't care about your future.
2. **Judy** (2019-08-19): Same here — my native-speaker tutor spoke more than I did. Helpful, but improvement was VERY SLOW. Insufficient practice time leads to bad results.
3. **Magda** (2019-08-19): Listening is a key foundation skill — one cannot contribute to a conversation if one cannot make sense of the other person. At least one hour of listening every day. Listening recycles vocabulary, improves pronunciation and comprehension. Download audiobooks, TED talks, films. Make listening to English part of your everyday life. [দীর্ঘ, প্রাঞ্জল কমেন্ট]
4. **Adis** (2019-08-19): Where do we get it?
5. **Jasmine** (2019-08-19): Several ideas: 1) immerse ourselves in English audio/video — after sufficient input we will automatically think in English; 2) search for English clubs in the neighbourhood (in my city Suzhou, China there are English groups in cafés/libraries — if none exist, set one up); 3) enroll in English courses — classmates of similar levels + teachers. If lucky, meet great teachers like Simon.
6. **arch1001face** (2019-08-19): From my experience: effort isn't enough — I studied 4 months in the Philippines + 6 months self-study but got band 5 because I lack the 'inside English ability'. I feel stuck and isolated; I hope to learn in England because I love its culture, architecture and football. I only follow Simon's methods.
7. **Lo** (2019-08-19): For introverts like me: listen to podcasts/YouTube on favourite topics every morning; read English news an hour a day then read transcripts aloud and record; after 2-3 days listen back and write down what you hear. Sing English songs in the shower, talk to myself in the mirror, communicate in English with family so we correct one another.
8. **Steven** (2019-08-19): Start small: switch your phone language to English, watch drama with subtitles, listen to the news, write a journal.
