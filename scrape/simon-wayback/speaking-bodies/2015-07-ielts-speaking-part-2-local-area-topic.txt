Practise answering the following part 2 question.

_**Describe a change that you would like to see in your local area.**_

_**You should say**_

_**\- what the change would be**_

_**\- who would be involved in making the change**_

_**\- how it would be done**_

_**\- and why you would like to see the change happen.**_

Tip:

Go through the 4 bullet points, and see if you can give a roughly equal amount of detail for each one, like I did in [this answer](https://ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2015/07/ielts-speaking-part-2-film-answer.html).
