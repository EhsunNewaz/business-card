Let's think about the following task from Cambridge IELTS 13. Which website would you choose, and why?

_Describe a website you use that helps you a lot in your work or studies._

_You should say_

_\- what the website is_

_\- how often you use the website_

_\- what information the website gives you_

_\- and explain how your work or studies would change if this website didn't exist._
