After last week's part 2 [answer](https://ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2015/07/ielts-speaking-part-2-film-answer.html), let's look at some part 3 questions about the same topic. Try answering the following questions:

1. Why do you think people like watching films?

2. Do you think films have changed since you were a child? How?

3. As the technology for home viewing improves, do you think people will stop going to the cinema in future?

**Tip:** Use the 'long answer' technique in [this lesson](https://ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2015/03/ielts-speaking-part-3-long-answer-technique.html).
