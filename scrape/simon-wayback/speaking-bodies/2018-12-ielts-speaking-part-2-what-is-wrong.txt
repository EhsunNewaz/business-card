Last Friday I asked you to look at this task:

_Describe an interesting house or apartment that you have visited._

Before I show you how I would answer, I have to say something about one student's response. This is how the student began answering the task:

_Well, living in an apartment is an overgrowing trend not only in my country but all over the world. We have our own apartment which is really awesome. By the way, I would like to talk about a new apartment/flat that I have recently visited._

Can you see what is wrong with the way this answer begins?
