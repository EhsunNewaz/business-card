Let's prepare an answer for the question below.

_**Describe a café that you have been to.**_

_**You should say**_

_**\- where it is**_

_**\- why you went there**_

_**\- what you ate or drank**_

_**\- and explain why you liked or disliked the café.**_

If you've seen 'Speaking lesson 4' in my [video course](http://subscriptions.viddler.com/SimonCorcoran), you will already have some good vocabulary for describing a restaurant and the staff in a restaurant e.g. welcoming, inviting, warm, approachable, attentive, nice atmosphere, make you feel at home... I'm sure we can use some of these words and phrases to describe a café.

I'll show you my answer next week.
