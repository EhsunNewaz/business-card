The last bullet point on the task card for speaking part 2 usually asks you to describe your feelings. Often, this last point contains the word 'why' e.g. explain _**why**_ you liked...

So, it's a good idea to prepare some good words and phrases that help you to express your feelings in a more interesting way. Instead of saying "it was nice", why not say "it was an unforgettable experience"?

**Study idea:**

Go back through my speaking lessons and search for full sample answers for part 2 (like the one I wrote [last Friday](https://ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2016/09/ielts-speaking-part-2-hurry-answer.html)). Go to the last bullet point in my answer, and copy any words or phrases that I use to express feelings.

**For example:**

\- I was filled with a sense of panic and fear

\- I felt so embarrassed

\- What I liked most about..... was...

\- I was amazed at how...

\- I enjoyed learning...
