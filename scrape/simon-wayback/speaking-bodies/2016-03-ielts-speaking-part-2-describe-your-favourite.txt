Have you prepared a description of your favourite film, book and website? I'm making a video lesson about these topics at the moment, and I'm trying to think of some vocabulary _**themes**_.

For the favourite website topic, I think we can say a lot about the _**features**_ and the website's _**usefulness**_. For the film or book, we can describe the **_plot_**, but can you think of any other vocabulary themes for these topics?
