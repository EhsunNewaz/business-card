In [this lesson](https://ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2014/08/ielts-speaking-part-1-games.html) we saw some part 1 questions about 'games' and I mainly talked about outdoor sports in my answers.

Here's a recent part 2 question that forces you to talk about indoor games, rather than outdoor sports:

_**Describe an indoor game that you enjoyed as a child. You should say**_

_**\- what the game was**_

_**\- where you played it**_

_**\- who played this game with you**_

_**\- and why you liked it**_

What games do you think we could talk about?
