If you've prepared the [six main topics](https://ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2016/04/ielts-speaking-part-2-outside-the-main-topics.html) well, you should be able to adapt and use your prepared ideas for most questions. Take the following question, for example:

_**Describe a beautiful sky that you watched**_

I would adapt my description of London (main topic: describe a place). I already have some good ideas: the spectacular views, the skyline, the iconic buildings, the landmarks etc. I could simply add that I watched the sunset from a 'vantage point' overlooking the city centre. This question now seems a lot easier!

Have another look through the topics that people shared in the comments below last week's [lesson](https://ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2016/04/ielts-speaking-part-2-outside-the-main-topics.html), and think about whether you could adapt your ideas from one of the six main topics.

_**Note:**_

Not every question can be answered by adapting the main topics. I'll cover some possible solutions to this problem in my next video lesson.
