Here's a useful part 1 topic from Cambridge IELTS book 14:

_Let's talk about the future..._

_1\. What job would you like to have ten years from now?_

_2\. How useful will English be for your future?_

_3\. How much travelling do you hope to do in the future?_

_4\. How do you think your life will change in the future?_

How would you answer these questions? Remember to give a direct answer and then explain it with a reason.
