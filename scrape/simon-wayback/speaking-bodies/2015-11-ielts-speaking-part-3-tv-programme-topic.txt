After the part 2 question that I showed you in last week's [lesson](https://ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2015/10/ielts-speaking-part-2-educa.html), the examiner would ask you some related questions in part 3 of the test.

Here are some part 3 questions about the 'educational TV programme' topic:

1. Do you think most people watch TV for education or for entertainment?

2. Should TV play a role in educating children? How?

3. How do you think TV viewing habits change as people get older?

_**Tip:**_

The secret to giving good answers in part 3 is to follow three simple steps: 1) give a direct answer 2) explain in more detail 3) give an example. There's also a 4th step if you want to say even more. Look at [this lesson](https://ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2015/03/ielts-speaking-part-3-long-answer-technique.html) to see an example.
