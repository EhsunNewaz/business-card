2015-10-25T09:51:50Z :: 2015-10-25T09:51:50Z :: Simon : ANSWERS FROM SIMON: / First, here are the problems: / 1. We don't say "at the weekend occasion", and we don't put capital letters after commas. Also, I wouldn't write "made me interested" - we can find a better way to say that. / 2. There needs to be a separation between the words "interested" and "that". "That program was..." is a new idea, and it would be best to start a new sentence. / 3. We don't say "I was appealed by". Also, I would change the word "then". / 4. "take care of healthy issue" is not an English way to express this idea. / 5. "Guidance" is an uncountable noun, so we don't use it as a plural. It's more normal in English to say "graduate students" or just "graduates", instead of "graduated students". Also, we have a present continuous verb (looking) which needs the auxiliary verb "are" i.e. "who ARE currently looking...". Finally, we don't say "their desire careers".
