HOWEVER, Part2 is even more important and the examiner will make adjustments to your score depending on the quality of your speech. As for Part3, as Simon mentioned, they are the type of questions that can boost your score and that's when the examiner will make his or her final decision. / Think about it, if you were to give "perfect" answers in Part1 and do poorly for the rest of the test, how can an eximaner determine your score based on only your Part1 answers? Remember, the score is the OVERALL score of the whole test, so you must speak well in all parts if you are looking for a high score. / Cheers, / Martin
