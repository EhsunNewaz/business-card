2014-05-09T19:42:56Z :: 2014-05-09T21:57:47Z :: Martin : In order to ADAPT something you have memorized to a new topic, the first thing you must do is to UNDERSTAND what you have actually memorized, and then MODIFY the original version to make it suitable and logical for the specific question. / In my opinion, "rote memorization" is becoming a big problem especially for students with low level of English as they tend to use memorized phrases, expressions, or even full sentences inappropriately for their answers (in both speaking and writing). When you see something nice from model essays/answers, make sure you do a bit of research on its usage (dictionary or Google) before spending the time to memorize it, otherwise I think that is just a waste of effort. / Have a nice weekend everyone!
