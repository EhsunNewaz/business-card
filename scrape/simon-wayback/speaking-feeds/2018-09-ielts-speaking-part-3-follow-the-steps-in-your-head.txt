2018-10-19T09:31:12Z :: 2018-10-19T09:31:12Z :: Simon : Here's a model answer for the first question: / Actually, I think a better way to deal with stress is to follow the steps in your head. I mean, imagine that you're doing something and you feel stressed. You need to slow down and think about what you're doing. / For example, if you're preparing for an exam and you feel stressed, you could take a deep breath, and then think about the first question, then the second, and so on. This way, you don't get overwhelmed by the whole task, and you just focus on one small step at a time.
