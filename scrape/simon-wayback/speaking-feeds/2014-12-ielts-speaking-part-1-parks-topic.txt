2014-12-07T12:56:16Z :: 2014-12-07T15:07:16Z :: Ellen : Hello Shano, / I don't think that you should talk about amusement parks. / In accordance with Wikipedia: 'An amusement park is a group of entertainment attractions, rides, and other events in a location for the enjoyment of large numbers of people', whereas 'A park is an area of open space provided for recreational use. It can be in its natural or semi-natural state, or planted, and is set aside for human enjoyment or for the protection of wildlife or natural habitats. It may consist of rocks, soil, water, flora and fauna and grass areas'. / As we can see, there is the clear difference between these two categories. / However, you can mention playgrounds, ball fields, lakes, swimming pools, picnic areas or skateparks as parts of a park and therefore this would be safer if you would like to talk about entertaining parts of parks.
