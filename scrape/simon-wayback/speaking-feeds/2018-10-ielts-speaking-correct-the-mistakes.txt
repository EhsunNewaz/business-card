2018-11-08T04:10:33Z :: 2018-11-08T04:10:33Z :: Simon : Here are the mistakes I found, and how to correct them: / 1) "I go to the gym every morning before I go to work" → "I go to the gym every morning before work" / 2) "She is more taller than me" → "She is taller than me" / 3) "I am interesting in photography" → "I am interested in photography" / 4) "I have been living here since five years" → "I have lived here for five years" / 5) "There are many people who likes to travel" → "There are many people who like to travel" / 6) "I didn't went to the cinema" → "I didn't go to the cinema" / 7) "The more you practice, the better you will be" → no error here - this is a good sentence! / 8) "He suggested me to go" → "He suggested that I should go" / 9) "I am used to wake up early" → "I am used to waking up early" / 10) "Each of the students have a book" → "Each of the students has a book"
