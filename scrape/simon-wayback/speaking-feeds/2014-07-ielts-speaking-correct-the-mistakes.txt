2014-07-05T15:54:39Z :: 2014-07-05T15:54:39Z :: Simon : SUGGESTED ANSWERS FROM SIMON / 1. Each of us helped TO ORGANISE this event. 2. I promised myself to try and spend more time with them IN (THE) FUTURE. / ('henceforth' is much too formal) / 3. We were all happy that we had a chance to be TOGETHER. / 4. HundredS of people came to her party to GIVE her special giftS. / 5. A celebration that I WILL never forget was... / (Only begin with "As for me" if you spoke about someone else first) / 6. We arranged many ENJOYABLE ACTIVITIES. / 7. Last month, my family HELD a birthday party for my FATHER. / 8. We HAD A party AT our house. / 9. All the family CAME together TO CELEBRATE this special event. / 10. There were quite a lot of people at the... / (Don't use long phrases to introduce each point. The phrase "Going on my next point which is who was present and what I need to emphasize here is that..." sounds very strange and will not help your score!)
