2018-11-18T04:34:11Z :: 2018-11-18T04:34:11Z :: Simon :: A useful strategy is to write down your key points, and to make sure you have enough to say for 2 minutes. The two minutes in part 2 is actually not that long, and you should be able to speak for 2 minutes on a familiar topic. Here's one more: / Describe a person you admire. You should say who this person is, how you know them, what they do, and explain why you admire them. / Here's a final one: / Describe a time you were proud of something you did. You should say what you did, when you did it, how you felt about it, and explain why you were proud of it.
