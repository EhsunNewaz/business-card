# IELTS Speaking / Grammar: a mixed conditional

- **Date:** 2020-02-07 | **আর্কাইভ লিংক:** [2020/02/ielts-speaking-grammar-a-mixed-conditional.html](https://web.archive.org/web/20200227115107id_/https://www.ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2020/02/ielts-speaking-grammar-a-mixed-conditional.html) | **Comments:** 4

## Content

Somebody asked me about this sentence from last Friday's speaking [lesson](https://ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2020/01/ielts-speaking-part-3-technology-topic.html):

- There _**would be**_ no computer programmers if the computer _**hadn't been invented**_.

The student asked me why I didn't follow the normal third conditional 'rule' and write "there would have been" instead of "there would be"? Did I make a mistake?

The answer is no, I didn't make a mistake. I wrote a mixed conditional sentence on purpose. Can you see why? I'll explain in tomorrow's lesson.
