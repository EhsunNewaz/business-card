## Comments

### Bui Quang Phuong — 2019-01-09T02:16:44Z

I would like to talk about my parents' house in the countryside. It is a small three-bedroom house with a big garden, and it's about 30 kilometers from the city center.

The house was built about twenty years ago and it is quite old now, but my parents keep it in good condition. The garden is the best part for me because I can grow some vegetables and flowers there, and my father has a small fish pond in the corner.

We usually spend a lot of time there, especially at weekends and during the Tet holiday. My mother cooks traditional food in the kitchen, and my father and I often sit in the yard and drink tea.

I love this place because it reminds me of my childhood. If I could make one change, I would add a bigger balcony so that the whole family could sit outside in the evening.
