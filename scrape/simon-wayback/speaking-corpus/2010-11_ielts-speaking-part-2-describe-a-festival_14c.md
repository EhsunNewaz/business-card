10. **Simon** (2010-11-14): Hi Hore boy, It's not an exact science because it also depends on whether you use the 'less common words/phrases' correctly and in a natural way. I've seen an official IELTS video in which a student only uses 3 or 4 less common phrases and still gets a 7 because he speaks in a fluent, natural way. The difference between 8 and 9 is quite small, but you can see in the descriptors for band 8 that the student can make some small mistakes in each area: look for the words "occasional inaccuracies", "occasional inappropriacies" or "hesitation to search for language". A band 9 student would speak fluently and naturally, and the only mistakes would be those that a native speaker might make.
