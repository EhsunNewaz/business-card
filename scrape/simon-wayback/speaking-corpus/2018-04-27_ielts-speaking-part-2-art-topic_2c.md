## Content

We looked at some part 1 questions about 'art' in last Friday's [lesson](https://ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2018/04/ielts-speaking-part-1-art-topic-negative-answers.html), so let's look at the same topic in part 2 of the speaking test.

_Describe a piece of art (a painting, drawing, sculpture or decorative object) that you like._ _You should say_

_\- what the piece of art is_

_\- where and when you saw it_

_\- what it looks like or what it shows_

_\- and explain why you like this piece of art._

**Tip:**

Maybe you're worried that you don't like art or you don't have any ideas for this topic. But the task doesn't tell you to describe a _famous_ piece of art. It would be fine to describe a picture that you drew yourself, or perhaps something that a child (a family member) drew at school.
