## Content

A few students said they had this topic in a recent exam. How would you answer?

_Let's talk about maps..._

_1\. Do you ever use maps?_

_2\. When do people usually need to use a map?_

_3\. Do you prefer electronic or paper maps?_

_4\. Do you ever ask people for directions instead of using a map?_

Remember to keep your answers short and simple. This topic might surprise you, but it isn't difficult.
