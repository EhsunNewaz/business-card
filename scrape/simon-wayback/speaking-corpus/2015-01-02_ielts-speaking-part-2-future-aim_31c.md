## Content

Here's a question that I think is relevant at this time of year:

_**Describe an aim or goal that you hope to achieve in the future.**_

_**You should say**_

_**\- what the aim is**_

_**\- when you hope to achieve it**_

_**\- what you need to do to reach your goal**_

_**\- and why this goal is important to you.**_
