9. **Simon** (2014-09-13): FROM SIMON: 1. not far from = within walking distance of 2. came together = gathered 3. they created promoted = they fostered 4. happy memories = fond memories 5. clear lasting memories = vivid memories 6. stressful = nerve-racking … Good work yenth. Note: We don't say "I felt nerve-racking". Use 'nerve-racking' to describe the situation e.g. "it was a nerve-racking experience". Also, 'nerve-racking' is not exactly the same as 'stressful'. It's better for situations that make you feel nervous stress e.g. the feeling you have before performing in public.
