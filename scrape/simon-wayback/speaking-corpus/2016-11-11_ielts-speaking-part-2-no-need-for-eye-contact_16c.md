# IELTS Speaking Part 2: no need for eye contact

- **Date:** 2016-11-11 | **আর্কাইভ লিংক:** [2016/11/ielts-speaking-part-2-no-need-for-eye-contact.html](https://web.archive.org/web/20190716171002id_/https://www.ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2016/11/ielts-speaking-part-2-no-need-for-eye-contact.html) | **Comments:** 16

## Content

When answering questions in parts 1 and 3, most people naturally make eye contact with the examiner (although it doesn't matter if you don't).

However, in part 2 you don't need to worry about eye contact _**at all**_. It's more important to look at the question and the notes you made.

Remember: the examiner _**will not**_ reduce your score for lack of eye contact, but he/she _**will**_ reduce your score if you don't answer the question well.
