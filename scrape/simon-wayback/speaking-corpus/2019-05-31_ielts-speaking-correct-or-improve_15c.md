## Content

Here are some sentences that people wrote about the [website for work](https://ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2019/05/ielts-speaking-part-2-website-for-work-topic.html) topic. Can you correct the mistakes and/or improve the sentences?

1. In the age of the internet, there are a plethora of options.

2. If this website doesn't exist, then definitely my study would be affected.

3. If YouTube will cease to exist, I find myself struggling to find the much-needed information.

4. I think it won't be possible for me to be more update and skilful if there would be nothing like Youtube.

5. Life also wouldn't be so easier and flexible like now than before when we used to depend much on CDs.

7. **HYS** (2019-06-01 (এডিট 2019-06-03)): 1. Improved: Don't use words like "plethora" in speaking part 1&2, or even part 3. They sound forced and pretentious. Examiners know you try to impress them, but using those big words may actually have a bad impact on your score. In the age of the internet, there are plenty of/ loads of options. 2. Corrected: Second conditional If this website didn't* exist, then definitely my study would be affected. 3. Corrected: Second conditional If YouTube ceased* to exist, I would* find myself struggling to find much-needed information. 4. Improved & Corrected: Second conditional & "nothing like" I think it would* be impossible* to keep myself updated with the lastest news and trends in my field if there was* no* Youtube if YouTube didn't exist. 5. Improved & Corrected: Second conditional Life also wouldn't* be so easy* and flexible like now. It would be like when we used to depend mainly on offline resources such as CDs, physical books and magazines, what have you.
