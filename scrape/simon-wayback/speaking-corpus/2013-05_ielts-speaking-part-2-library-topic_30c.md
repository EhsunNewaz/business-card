9. **Simon** (2013-05-11): : FROM SIMON: Here are my suggestions: 1. The big problem is that we don't say "no any" in English. Just say something like: There was no room for people to sit and read borrowed books. 2. The problem was the use of "where" and "there" together. Correction: I am going to talk about a library where I used to go every day during my student years. 3. Strange use of "accustomed". I'd say: I went there regularly because I found it really useful. 4. You need to say "one of the biggest libraries." 5. You can't say "I used to went". Also, I prefer "within walking distance". Correction: I used to go there when I was a student as it was within walking distance from my campus.
