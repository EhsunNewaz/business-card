# IELTS Speaking: work or study?

- **Date:** 2018-12-28 | **আর্কাইভ লিংক:** [2018/12/ielts-speaking-work-or-study.html](https://web.archive.org/web/20191017100436id_/https://www.ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2018/12/ielts-speaking-work-or-study.html) | **Comments:** 4

## Content

These are common questions at the start of the speaking test:

1. Do you work or are you a student?

2. Do you like your job / course? Why / why not?

Example answers:

1. Before I came to England I was working as an accountant, but at the moment I'm studying English because I hope to do a Master's here.

2. Yes, I really like my job because it's interesting and I work with a great team of people. _**OR:**_ Yes, I enjoy studying English, but my real aim is to do a Master's and then find a job in an international company.

_**Note:**_

Don't ask the examiner: "Do you mean in my country or here in England?" - it doesn't matter. Don't worry if you have a job and study at the same time - you can mention both.
