## Content

A student sent me this recent exam question. How would you answer it?

_Describe a place of entertainment (e.g. theatre, cinema, sports centre) that you would like to have where you live. You should say_

_\- what this place would be_

_\- where it should be_

_\- whom it would be for, or who would use it_

_\- and why you think this place of entertainment is needed_
