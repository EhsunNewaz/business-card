## Content

Many people find it difficult to keep talking for 2 minutes because they give very quick answers to one or two of the bullet points on the task card.

Take last week's question for example:

_**Describe a long walk that you enjoyed.**_

_**You should say**_

_**\- when you went on this walk**_

_**\- where it took place**_

_**\- what you saw while you were walking**_

_**\- and explain why you enjoyed the walk.**_

It would be easy to answer the first two bullet points very quickly:

_"Last summer I walked along a beach during my holiday in..."_

The problem now is that we need to spend almost the full 2 minutes on the last two bullet points. It would be better if we could expand on all four points, like I did in [this lesson](https://ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2015/10/ielts-speaking-part-2-educa.html).

**Task:**

Can you invent some more information to expand on the short answer that I gave above (about walking along a beach last summer)? Try to invent as much as you can about the "when" and the "where".
