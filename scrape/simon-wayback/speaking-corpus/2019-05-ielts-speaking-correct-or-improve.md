### HYS — 2019-06-01T14:51:59Z (edited 2019-06-03T09:53:00Z)

1. Improved: Don't use words like "plethora" in speaking part 1&2, or even part 3. They sound forced and pretentious. Examiners know you try to impress them, but using those big words may actually have a bad impact on your score.

In the age of the internet, there are plenty of/ loads of options.

2. Corrected: Second conditional

If this website didn't* exist, then definitely my study would be affected.

3. Corrected: Second conditional

If YouTube ceased* to exist, I would* find myself struggling to find much-needed information.

4. Improved & Corrected: Second conditional & "nothing like"

I think it would* be impossible* to keep myself updated with the lastest news and trends in my field if there was* no* Youtube

if YouTube didn't exist.

5. Improved & Corrected: Second conditional

Life also wouldn't* be so easy* and flexible like now. It would be like when we used to depend mainly on offline resources such as CDs, physical books and magazines, what have you.
