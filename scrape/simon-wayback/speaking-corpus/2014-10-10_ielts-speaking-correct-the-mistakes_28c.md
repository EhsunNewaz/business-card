## Content

Here are some sentences that students wrote below last week's [lesson](https://ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2014/10/ielts-speaking-part-1-house-or-apartment.html). Can you correct the mistake(s) in each one?

1. I would want the walls to be painted in bright colors, arrange the furniture with some good interior designers.

2. If I am well off, I would like to move into much more bigger house.

3. If I had enough money, I will buy some more household appliances.

4. My favourite room is my living room because most of the time I spent in that room.

5. It goes without saying that I am quite passionate about my bedroom.

No grammar mistakes, but "it goes without saying" seems strange and inappropriate here - why does it go without saying?
