4. **Simon** (2012-01-05): : Hi Mary, I'm afraid there is no special technique or fast way to improve your score. Just keep working hard, follow the lessons on this site, use the Cambridge books for test practice, and take some lessons with a teacher who can check your work and highlight your mistakes. ... Hi KP, I definitely recommend speaking in the first person (I think...). Using "It is believed..." when someone asks YOU a question sounds very strange. I didn't really mean that you should avoid ALL big words, just that you shouldn't memorise strange words that you find in the dictionary. If there is a question about a topic that you have read about in a newspaper, you might be able to use some RELEVANT big words that you read in the newspaper article. This advice is even more important for the speaking test - it sounds very strange when students use memorised 'big' words in normal conversation.
