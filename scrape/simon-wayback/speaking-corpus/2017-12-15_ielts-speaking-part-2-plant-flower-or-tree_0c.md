## Content

Here are two tasks that are similar to a recent exam question:

_1) Describe a plant, flower or tree that is famous or important in your country._

_You should say_

_\- what type of plant, flower or tree it is_

_\- where it grows or is usually seen_

_\- how it is used in your country_

_\- and why it is famous or important_

_2) Describe a plant, flower or tree that you like._

_You should say_

_\- what type of plant, flower or tree it is_

_\- where it grows or is usually seen_

_\- when you first saw it_

_\- and why you like it_

The two tasks are almost the same, so let's choose the same plant, flower or tree for both. Which one would you choose to describe? You'll see my choice next week.
