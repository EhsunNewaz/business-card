3. **Simon** (2010-09-30): Hi Martin, I'd say that part 2 and 3 are both equally important. Part 2 can be easier for some students because there are no probing questions. Part 3 can be more difficult because students are asked to go deeper into the topic. They are expected to analyse, compare and give opinions. Perhaps your student is reluctant to express her views or finds it difficult to be critical. Many students are worried that they will upset the examiner if they express something controversial. Very few students are able to really expand on a topic with opinions, critical analysis and real examples. Try to push/train your student to do this. She mustn't worry about what the examiner might think of her opinions - if the discussion is interesting and real, she's more likely to get a high score. Hope this helps Simon
