3. **Simon** (2010-03-06): Hi Basheer, This is an interesting question. If the examiner asks you about something you don't know/like/care about in PART 1 of the test (short interview questions), you can just say that you don't really like it and give a reason e.g. "I don't often watch films or go to the cinema because I'm too busy studying." If the PART 2 question (2 minute presentation) is about something you don't like, you should try your best - see my advice below. Maybe I was a strict examiner, but I never changed the question - I don't think examiners are really allowed to do this, so you were very lucky! My advice: I'm sure you have family, children or friends who watch films. You could say: "I don't really like watching films, but my children love watching 'Shrek'" (for example). Then describe the film that your children like, say why they like it and explain why you think it's a nice film for children. Or you could describe a film that is popular in your country, even if you haven't seen it. Choose a film now so that you are ready for this question. Do a Google search or search for reviews on Amazon. Write down the ideas you find, and practise with a friend. Hope this helps, Simon
