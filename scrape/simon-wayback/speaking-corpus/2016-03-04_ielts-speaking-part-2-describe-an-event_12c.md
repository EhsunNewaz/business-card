## Content

I've now published my latest video lesson (speaking lesson 6) at the bottom of [this webpage](http://subscriptions.viddler.com/SimonCorcoran). In the lesson, I look at 4 types of event:

1. party

2. festival

3. sports event

4. concert or musical event

Which type of event would you find the most difficult to describe? Can you think of any vocabulary 'themes' that could be used to describe any event?
