## Content

This is an interesting set of questions from Cambridge IELTS book 11:

_Let's talk about names..._

_1) How did your parents choose your name?_

_2) Does your name have any special meaning?_

_3) Is your name common or unusual in your country?_

_4) If you could change your name, would you?_

Remembering the advice that I gave [last week](https://ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/2016/10/ielts-speaking-you-cant-get-a-high-score-in-part-1.html), try answering these questions (aloud) without hesitating too much. It's fine to give negative answers or to say that you don't know the answer, but you should still explain why.
