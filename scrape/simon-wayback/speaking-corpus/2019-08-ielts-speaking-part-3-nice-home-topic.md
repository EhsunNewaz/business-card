### A_Sayed — 2019-09-28T13:52:19Z

Please, could you correct my answers?

1- What do you think a nice home should be like?

A nice home is one that provides comfort, privacy, and all the basic amenities that a family needs to live a relaxed and happy life.

2- Do you think people in your country live in nice houses?

Actually, most people in my country live in flats or apartments, and these homes are not always as spacious or well-designed as I'd like them to be, but they are usually adequate for basic living.

3- What is the most important room in a home?

In my opinion, the kitchen is the most important room in a home, because it's the place where the family comes together to share meals and spend time together, and it's also where food is prepared.

4- Would you prefer to live in a house or an apartment?

I would prefer to live in a house because houses usually have more outdoor space and offer a quieter living environment, which I find very relaxing.

5- Do you think your home will be different in the future?

I think my home will become more technology-driven, with smart devices and automated systems controlling lighting, temperature, and security to make daily life easier.

6- Do you think people spend too much time at home nowadays?

I don't think so. Many people are busy with work, studies, and other responsibilities, so they usually don't have much free time to spend at home.

7- Do you think that buying a home is a good investment?

I believe buying a home is a good investment because property prices tend to rise over time, and owning a home provides long-term financial security.

8- How do you think people will live in the future?

I think people will live in more compact, sustainable homes equipped with smart technology, and many of them will work and study from home most of the time.

9- Is it better to live in a big house or a small apartment?

It depends on a person's lifestyle and family size, but I think a small, well-designed apartment is more practical for most people today because it's easier to maintain and more affordable.

10- Do you think people will continue to live in big houses in the future?

Probably not, as land is limited and housing costs are rising, so most people will likely choose smaller homes or apartments that are more affordable.
