## Content

Here's a recent question that a student told me about:

_**Describe an enjoyable day out in the countryside that you had.**_

_**You should say**_

_**\- where you went on this day out**_

_**\- who you were with**_

_**\- what you did**_

_**\- and explain why you enjoyed the day**_

Feel free to share your ideas in the comments area below this lesson. I'll continue with this topic next Friday.
