This video by IELTS Simon provides a comprehensive guide on how to approach True, False, Not Given and Yes, No, Not Given questions in the IELTS Reading test. The instructor explains that despite the difference in terminology, both types of questions should be handled using the same core strategy.

Core Exam Techniques:

Keyword Technique (0:34-0:57): Underline keywords in the question, search for them in the passage, and compare the statements carefully.
Order of Answers (10:02-10:23): Answers for these question types always appear in the correct sequential order within the passage.
Vocabulary Focus (11:35-11:42, 17:31-17:49): Recognizing synonyms (e.g., intricate vs complex, logs vs wood) is crucial for identifying the correct answer.
Understanding the Options:

True/Yes: The information is confirmed in the passage (0:19-0:21).
False/No: The information in the passage contradicts the statement (0:21-0:23, 9:21-9:36).
Not Given: There is insufficient information in the text to confirm or deny the statement (8:54-9:00, 9:36-9:43).
