This video is the ninth and final lesson in a series covering the nine types of questions found in the IELTS Reading test. In this session, the focus is on matching sentence endings.

Key Techniques for Matching Sentence Endings:
Preparation: Always read the title of the passage first to understand the topic (1:11-1:36). Underline keywords in the provided sentence endings before starting the questions to distinguish between the options (1:49-3:02).
Strategy: Work through the questions in order, as the answers generally appear in the same sequence within the passage (3:06-3:23).
Keyword Matching: After identifying a question, read the passage at a normal speed to locate the relevant section (3:54-4:01). Look for synonyms and paraphrased information that match the keywords you underlined in both the question and the potential sentence endings (5:55-6:04, 10:27-11:15).
Practical Example:
The lesson walks through a sample passage about the Rorschach test, demonstrating how to link question stems to their correct endings by identifying corresponding assumptions, aims, and purposes within the text (3:26-9:51).
